How Folder Organizer works

Use it in three steps

  1. Choose a source folder. This is the folder the app scans.
  2. Scan the folder. Review the totals, years, and warnings before organizing.
  3. Choose Move or Copy. Move reorganizes the source folder. Copy leaves the originals where they are and asks for a destination folder.

What gets organized

Photos and videos are placed in Photos/<year> and Videos/<year>. The scan searches through subfolders, but skips folders named Photos or Videos so an organized folder is not scanned again.

Supported formats are JPG, JPEG, HEIC, and PNG photos, plus MOV, MP4, and M4V videos. Other file types are shown in the scan but are not organized.

Warnings to check

  • Missing capture date: when reliable photo or video metadata is unavailable, the file modified date is used for its year. That may not be the original capture year.
  • Duplicate filename: files with the same name are not overwritten. The later destination gets a suffix such as (1) or (2).
  • Zero-byte file: empty files are skipped and are not copied or moved.
  • Unreadable file: files whose size or date cannot be read are skipped from organization.
  • Move changes the source: the default Move option removes successfully organized files from their original locations. Use Copy when you need to preserve them.
  • Scan before organizing: the app organizes the results from the last scan. Scan again after changing the source folder or its contents.
